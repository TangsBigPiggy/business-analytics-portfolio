Inventory exposure, not lost revenue
Payment status changes the meaning of the headline. Non Refund exhibits an unusual 99.8% non-materialization pattern and requires source/process validation before commercial interpretation.
Exposure is concentrated. In the full H2 scope, Online TA represents 58.3% of No Deposit bookings but 75.8% of failed No Deposit room nights.
Timing separates two operating problems. Early cancellations create a possible recovery window; late cancellations and no-shows leave much less time to respond.
